I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Research Fellow in the Department of Civil, Environmental & Geomatic Engineering at University College London (UCL), working with Prof Carmine Galasso.

My research focuses on natural hazard risk and resilience in a changing climate, with particular emphasis on rainfall-induced landslides, multi-hazard interactions, probabilistic risk assessment, and transport infrastructure resilience.

Prior to joining UCL, I worked as a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Hong Kong. I received my PhD in Geological Resources and Geological Engineering from Tongji University in 2025. During my doctoral studies, I was a visiting PhD researcher at Waseda University, Japan, from 2022 to 2024.

## Research Interests

- Climate change and rainfall hazards
- Landslide mechanics and risk
- Multi-hazard risk assessment
- Infrastructure risk and resilience
