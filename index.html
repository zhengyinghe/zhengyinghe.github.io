I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Civil, Environmental and Geomatic Engineering at University College London (UCL), working with Professor Carmine Galasso.

My research focuses on natural hazard risk and resilience in a changing climate, with particular emphasis on rainfall-induced landslides, multi-hazard interactions, probabilistic risk assessment, and transport infrastructure resilience.

Prior to joining UCL, I worked as a Research Associate (Postdoctoral Researcher) at the University of Hong Kong. I received my PhD in Geological Resources and Geological Engineering from Tongji University in 2025. During my doctoral studies, I was a visiting PhD researcher at Waseda University, Japan, from 2022 to 2024.

Research Interests

  • Climate change impacts on natural hazards
  • Rainfall-induced landslides and slope stability
  • Stochastic modelling of non-stationary rainfall
  • Vegetation–soil–water interactions
  • Multi-hazard risk and fragility assessment
  • Transport infrastructure risk and resilience
